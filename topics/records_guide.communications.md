# Communication records
<!-- id: topic:records_guide.communications -->

Part of: [SuiteScript records guide](records_guide.md)

Communication records hold correspondence and annotations that are attached to other records.

## Record types in this group

| Record ID | Reference page |
|---|---|
| `message` | [message](../gen/records/message.md) |
| `note` | [note](../gen/records/note.md) |

The spec states nothing more about this group than its members. Field IDs, sublists and search
columns are on the record pages linked above; which channels (SuiteScript, SOAP, CSV, Connect, REST)
support each record is in the [SuiteCloud record support catalog](../gen/catalogs/suitecloud_record_support.md).
