# Marketing records
<!-- id: topic:records_guide.marketing -->

Part of: [SuiteScript records guide](records_guide.md)

Marketing records support campaigns (with their audiences, channels, offers and responses),
promotions and coupon codes, lead sources and the email templates used to reach customers. The
group has 16 record types.

## Record types in this group

| Record ID | Reference page |
|---|---|
| `campaign` | [campaign](../gen/records/campaign.md) |
| `campaignaudience` | [campaignaudience](../gen/records/campaignaudience.md) |

| `campaignoffer` | [campaignoffer](../gen/records/campaignoffer.md) |

How each record is supported in SuiteScript, SOAP, CSV, Connect and REST is in the
[SuiteCloud record support catalog](../gen/catalogs/suitecloud_record_support.md). The source gives
no scripting rules that apply to the whole group; per-record facts are on the record pages.
